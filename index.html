Mid-January is the worst of it at home and the best of it down there. Same week, three different versions of warm.

What every version includes
  • No alarm clock. Nothing at all happens before 10am
  • A book and a lounger with nothing scheduled around them
  • Something cold that arrives before you have to ask twice
  • Nothing to walk to. Sand, water and bar within a few steps
  • A bagel and strong coffee. Savory, no pastry, no eggs
  • One night that goes late, properly late, music and all
Already handled
  • Nonstop out of Seattle, plus transfers, door to sand
  • Waterfront hotel, straight out of the door onto the sand
  • Dinner reservations for the night that deserves one
  • Vegetarian everywhere, every meal, no negotiating at the table
  • Zero planning asked of you beyond this page
  • Packing list: swimsuit, one good dress, sunglasses, novel

Pick this if

Zero friction. No wallet, no prices on the menu, no planning. Dry heat, big desert coastline, and a party a few steps from the room.

Worth knowing

A family resort with adults-only zones, not an adults-only hotel. The January sea runs cool, so this is more pool than ocean.
